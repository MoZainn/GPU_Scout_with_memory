# RX 7600

## Overview

AMD's RX 7600 is the direct budget competitor to the RTX 4060, built on RDNA 3. It generally trades blows with the 4060 in rasterized (non-ray-traced) gaming performance, often at a slightly lower price, while trailing in ray tracing and AI/upscaling tooling.

---

## Specifications

Vendor: AMD

Architecture: RDNA 3

Launch Year: 2023

VRAM: 8 GB GDDR6

Compute Units: 32

Stream Processors: 2048

Memory Bandwidth: 288 GB/s

TDP: 165W

PCIe Interface: PCIe 4.0 x8

---

## AI Workloads

### LLM Inference

AMD GPUs generally have a smaller, less mature local-AI software ecosystem than NVIDIA (CUDA has broader tooling support). ROCm support exists but historically has more setup friction than NVIDIA's CUDA/cuDNN stack.

8GB VRAM caps this card to small quantized models, similar to the RTX 4060.

Not recommended as a primary AI card if avoiding setup friction is a priority — NVIDIA remains the safer choice for most local AI tooling as of this writing.

---

## Gaming

### 1080p Gaming

Strong rasterized performance, competitive with or slightly ahead of the RTX 4060 in many non-RT titles, at a typically lower price.

### 1440p Gaming

Workable at medium-high settings; 8GB VRAM is a similar constraint to the RTX 4060 in newer titles.

### 4K Gaming

Not recommended.

### Ray Tracing

Weaker ray tracing performance relative to NVIDIA's equivalent tier — AMD's RT hardware still trails NVIDIA's Tensor/RT core implementation. FSR (AMD's upscaler) is used to compensate, and works across NVIDIA/AMD/Intel GPUs, unlike DLSS which is NVIDIA-exclusive.

---

## Power and System Requirements

165W TDP, typically a single 8-pin PCIe power connector. 550-650W PSU is sufficient.

---

## Value Positioning

Best suited for: budget 1080p gamers who prioritize rasterized performance-per-dollar and don't plan on doing local AI work, where NVIDIA's ecosystem still has a meaningful edge. A reasonable RTX 4060 alternative if ray tracing and AI aren't priorities.

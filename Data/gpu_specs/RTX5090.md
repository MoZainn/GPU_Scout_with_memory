# Overview

The NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 is NVIDIA's flagship consumer GPU based on the Blackwell architecture. It succeeds the RTX 4090 and delivers substantial improvements in AI inference, ray tracing, memory bandwidth, and gaming performance. It is designed for enthusiasts, AI developers, content creators, and professionals requiring maximum GPU performance.

# Reasoning Facts

The RTX 5090 has more VRAM than the RTX 4090.

More VRAM allows larger AI models to fit entirely into GPU memory.

GDDR7 memory provides higher bandwidth than GDDR6X.

Higher memory bandwidth improves AI inference speed.

Tensor Cores accelerate AI workloads.

CUDA cores accelerate general GPU computation.

DLSS 4 improves gaming performance using AI.

The RTX 5090 is generally faster than the RTX 4090 in AI and gaming workloads.

Large language models benefit from increased VRAM and bandwidth.

The RTX 5090 is one of the best consumer GPUs for local AI development.

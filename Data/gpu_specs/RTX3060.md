# RTX 3060 (12GB)

## Overview

The RTX 3060 is a previous-generation (Ampere) GPU that remains one of the most recommended budget cards, especially on the used market, largely because of its unusually generous 12GB VRAM for its price tier.

---

## Specifications

Vendor: NVIDIA

Architecture: Ampere

Launch Year: 2021

VRAM: 12 GB GDDR6

CUDA Cores: 3584

Tensor Cores: 112

RT Cores: 28

Memory Bandwidth: 360 GB/s

TDP: 170W

PCIe Interface: PCIe 4.0 x16

---

## AI Workloads

### LLM Inference

The 12GB VRAM punches above its price class for local AI experimentation.

Suitable Models:

- Llama 7B / 8B
- Mistral 7B
- Llama 13B (quantized)

A frequently recommended "budget AI card" for exactly this reason — more VRAM than many newer, more expensive 8GB cards.

### Stable Diffusion

Comfortably handles SD 1.5 and SDXL at reasonable batch sizes thanks to the 12GB buffer.

---

## Gaming

### 1080p Gaming

Very strong, high settings, high frame rates in the vast majority of current titles.

### 1440p Gaming

Good performance at medium-high settings; the 12GB VRAM helps it age better than 8GB cards from the same era or newer.

### 4K Gaming

Not recommended as a primary use case — raw compute (CUDA core count, memory bandwidth) is the limiting factor here, not VRAM.

### Ray Tracing

Entry-to-mid ray tracing performance. Light RT effects with DLSS are playable; heavier ray tracing struggles.

---

## Power and System Requirements

170W TDP, typically one 8-pin PCIe power connector. A 550-650W PSU is sufficient.

---

## Value Positioning

Best suited for: budget 1080p/1440p gamers and anyone wanting a low-cost entry into local AI experimentation. Frequently the best price-to-VRAM ratio in the budget segment, especially on the used market, which is why it remains a commonly recommended card years after release.
